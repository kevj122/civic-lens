# civic-lens

**An open-source, privacy-first browser layer for human dignity, historical context, and anti-racism media literacy.**

> *"Where, after all, do universal human rights begin? In small places, close to home."*

---

## Why This Exists

Algorithms and AI systems increasingly shape what people read and believe online—frequently amplifying misinformation, dehumanizing conspiracy theories, and racism in all its forms (including antisemitism, anti-Black racism, Islamophobia, and xenophobia).

**[Project Name]** is an initiative to build a free, transparent, community-governed browser extension (and eventual open-source browser layer) that brings settled international human rights standards directly into everyday browsing—protecting vulnerable communities and educating users without spying on them or resorting to blunt censorship.

## Core Principles

1. **Context Over Censorship:** Distinguish between promoting hatred and reporting on, studying, or challenging it. Surface verified historical context and media-literacy cues rather than simply silencing speech.
2. **Anchored in International Human Rights:** Rules are grounded in post-WWII institutions and frameworks built to prevent atrocities—not corporate profit motives or shifting politics. See our [CHARTER.md](./CHARTER.md).
3. **Zero Surveillance (Privacy by Design):** Analysis happens locally on the user's device wherever possible. We do not log, track, or sell browsing history.
4. **All Forms of Racism Opposed:** Built to counter racism, antisemitism, religious bigotry, and dehumanizing conspiracy narratives equally and consistently.
5. **Radically Open:** Open-source code, open classification criteria, and open community governance.
