# Contributing to Civic-lens

Thank you for your interest in helping build a safer, more educational web! Because this project sits at the intersection of technology, history, and human rights, we value **non-technical contributors** just as much as software engineers.

## Ways to Contribute

### 1. Historians, Educators & Researchers
- Suggest well-documented historical tropes, conspiracy narratives, or forms of hate speech that are frequently misunderstood online.
- Write concise, calm, strictly factual "Context Card" explanations backed by primary archives, museums, or peer-reviewed sources.
- Review proposed entries to ensure they remain neutral, accurate, and non-preachy.

### 2. Developers & AI Ethics Engineers
- Help build the lightweight, privacy-preserving browser extension (`extension/`).
- Design local/on-device matching and lightweight classification tools that require zero server logging.
- Improve accessibility and user experience for schools and everyday browsers.

### 3. Teachers, Librarians & Community Advocates
- Share feedback on what young people and students are encountering online.
- Help shape pilot testing and usability in educational settings.

## Community Standards
All participants are expected to engage respectfully, argue in good faith, and uphold the principles in our [CHARTER.md](./CHARTER.md). Harassment, bad-faith trolling, or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

## Getting Started
1. Click the **Issues** tab at the top of this repository.
2. Open a new issue titled `Intro: [Your Name / Background]` and share how you'd like to get involved!
