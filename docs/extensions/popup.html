Open Context Layer (v0.1)

Active • 100% Local Processing

An open-source prototype bringing verified historical context and human rights standards directly into your browser.

  • Context over censorship: Educates without hiding pages.
  • Zero surveillance: Nothing is logged or sent to servers.
  • All racism opposed: Covers antisemitism, racism, and xenophobia equally.

Tip: Test it by visiting a Wikipedia article on "The Protocols of the Elders of Zion", "Blood libel", or "Scientific racism".
