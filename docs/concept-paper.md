## 1. Executive Summary
Modern web browsers and AI-driven platforms shape how billions of people learn, communicate, and form beliefs. Yet the algorithms powering much of the online world are optimized for attention and engagement—frequently amplifying misinformation, dehumanizing conspiracy theories, and racism in all its forms, including antisemitism, anti-Black racism, Islamophobia, and xenophobia.

**[Project Name]** proposes building a free, open-source, privacy-respecting browser layer (starting as a lightweight browser extension and scaling toward a dedicated open-source browser) designed to uphold human dignity online. Rather than relying on opaque corporate moderation or crude censorship, the project will translate settled post-World War II international human rights standards into transparent, auditable technology—educating everyday users, protecting vulnerable communities, and preventing AI systems from amplifying hate.

---

## 2. The Problem
Online racism and hate rarely spread in a vacuum. They rely on two distinct dynamics:
* **Initiators:** Actors who deliberately generate and spread dehumanizing propaganda, harassment, and extremist narratives.
* **Followers:** Everyday users—particularly young people—who encounter coded tropes, historical distortions, or conspiracy theories out of context and repeat or absorb them without recognizing their origins or real-world harm.

Current digital infrastructure fails to address both groups effectively:
1. **Commercial Incentives:** Engagement-driven algorithms often reward outrage and polarization rather than accuracy or empathy.
2. **Blunt Moderation vs. Context:** Automated keyword filters frequently fail to distinguish between *promoting* hatred and *reporting on, studying, or challenging* it—often censoring journalism and anti-racist education while missing coded "dog whistles."
3. **The Enforcement Gap:** While international institutions have established clear ethical principles against online hatred and discriminatory AI, those frameworks remain largely voluntary and absent from the tools people use daily to browse the web.

## 3. Core Values & Institutional Foundation
Rather than inventing subjective rules controlled by a private company or a single government, **[Project Name]** will anchor its open specification in the international institutions and treaties created specifically to prevent the recurrence of mass atrocities:

* **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR) & International Human Rights Law:** Affirming equal human dignity while balancing freedom of expression against incitement to hatred, discrimination, and violence.
* **The UN Rabat Plan of Action:** Providing a nuanced, context-based standard (examining speaker intent, context, reach, and likelihood of real-world harm) so the software distinguishes malicious incitement from legitimate education, journalism, and political debate.
* **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence:** Ensuring any AI components prioritize non-discrimination, fairness, transparency, and human oversight.
* **UN Global Principles for Information Integrity:** Rejecting the monetization and algorithmic amplification of hate speech and disinformation.

## 4. Technical Vision: How It Works

### Phase 1: Open-Source Browser Extension
Building a web rendering engine from scratch is unnecessary and cost-prohibitive. **[Project Name]** will begin as an open-source browser extension compatible with major browsers (Firefox, Chrome, Edge), allowing rapid prototyping, low cost, and immediate testing.

### Phase 2: Shared Open-Source Browser
Once validated, the tool can be integrated into a dedicated, open-source browser fork (built on Gecko/Firefox or Chromium) designed specifically for schools, libraries, families, and civic-minded users.

### Key Features
1. **Context Over Censorship (Educational Layer):** When a user encounters well-documented racist tropes, Holocaust denial, or dehumanizing conspiracy narratives online, the tool can surface non-intrusive, verified historical context and links to trusted educational archives—helping "followers" recognize manipulation in real time.
2. **Opt-In Harm Reduction:** Configurable filters enabling schools, parents, and individuals to block overt hate speech, harassment networks, and extremist recruitment sites.
3. **AI Safety Guardrails:** Ensuring integrated search or summarization tools are actively tested ("red-teamed") to avoid generating, amplifying, or sanitizing racist stereotypes.
4. **Privacy-by-Design:** A tool built on human dignity must never become a surveillance tool. Analysis will run locally on the user’s device wherever possible, with strict zero-logging policies so browsing history is never tracked, stored, or sold.

## 5. Governance & Public-Interest Model
To ensure **[Project Name]** cannot be bought out, monetized through surveillance ads, or hijacked for political censorship:
* **Legal Structure:** Established as an independent non-profit organization (e.g., a UK Community Interest Company or charitable foundation) rather than a venture-backed startup.
* **Radical Transparency:** All source code, AI evaluation criteria, and filtering rules will be open-source and publicly auditable, with a clear, independent appeals process for errors.
* **Multi-Stakeholder Advisory Board:** Guided jointly by human rights lawyers, Holocaust and anti-racism educators, librarians, teachers, and AI ethics researchers.

## 6. Pilot Strategy & Immediate Next Steps
Rather than competing directly in the commercial browser market on day one, **[Project Name]** will pilot where the need is clearest: **schools, public libraries, and youth media-literacy programs**.

**Immediate 6-Month Roadmap:**
1. **Coalition Building:** Convene a small founding group of 3–5 partners spanning open-source software development, AI ethics, and anti-racism/historical education.
2. **Drafting the Open Rulebook:** Translate the UN Rabat Plan of Action and UNESCO AI Ethics principles into an initial, testable classification rubric.
3. **Proof-of-Concept Prototype:** Build a minimal open-source browser extension demonstrating context cards for a small set of well-documented historical hate tropes while preserving 100% user privacy.
4. **Seed Funding & Pilot:** Apply for digital public infrastructure / tech-for-good grants to run a small pilot with partner teachers and students.
