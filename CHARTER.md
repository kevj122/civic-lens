# Ethical & Human Rights Charter

## 1. Purpose
Civic-lens exists to uphold human dignity online, counter all forms of racism and dehumanization, and ensure AI and digital tools do not amplify hatred.

## 2. Normative Foundations
Rather than relying on arbitrary moderation rules, our framework draws from settled international human rights standards established to prevent mass atrocities:

- **Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, 1948):** Affirming equal dignity and rights for all people without distinction of race, religion, colour, or national origin.
- **UN Rabat Plan of Action:** Evaluating expression carefully based on context, speaker intent, reach, and likelihood of real-world harm—ensuring legitimate journalism, historical education, and political debate are protected.
- **UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence:** Requiring transparency, fairness, non-discrimination, and human oversight in any automated classification.
- **UN Global Principles for Information Integrity:** Rejecting the algorithmic amplification and monetization of hate and disinformation.

## 3. Distinguishing Hate from Education
This project will never treat the mere presence of sensitive historical terms as hate speech. Discussing the Holocaust, slavery, colonialism, racism, or Middle Eastern politics critically and educationally is vital to learning. Our tools are designed to identify dehumanizing tropes and incitement in context, and to empower users with verified educational sources.

## 4. Strict Privacy Commitment
A tool built to protect human dignity must never become an instrument of surveillance:
- **No tracking:** User browsing histories must never be collected, logged, profiled, or monetized.
- **Local-first processing:** Page analysis and context matching will run locally on the user's device wherever technically feasible.

## 5. Governance & Accountability
All rules, datasets, and code used by this project must remain publicly auditable. Errors and false positives must have a clear, transparent community review and correction process.
